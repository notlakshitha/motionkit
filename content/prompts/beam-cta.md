# Beam call to action

Build an original closing band. Hairline beams travel across a dark field. No particles, no 3D.

## Scene

Background `#080808`. An absolutely positioned SVG, `preserveAspectRatio="none"`, holds three diagonal lines:

- Brass
- Paper white at lower opacity
- Sea glass

Each line uses a dashed stroke and animates `stroke-dashoffset` at a slightly different duration so they do not march together. Add a soft brass radial glow behind the type.

## Copy

Centered on large screens, left on a short preview:

- Eyebrow `LAST LIGHT`
- Serif headline `Leave a light on for the next reel.`
- One sentence that says the beams are decoration and the invitation is the thing that needs a click
- Button `Start a screening`

## Reduced motion

Stop the dash animation. The lines remain drawn. The glow and the type do not move.

## Done when

The beams feel like light traveling, the headline is the focus, and reduced motion leaves a still poster.
